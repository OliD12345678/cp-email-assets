Your Pro-Rated Final Payment

If your final draft hits between 9/16 and 10/14, your final draft has been automatically pro-rated based on the number of days remaining before we close.

For example, if your normal membership is $149/month, that breaks down to about $4.97 per day.

If your payment is due on 9/28/26 and there are 17 days remaining until October 15th, your final payment would be $84.43 instead of the full $149.

So you’re only being charged for the days you’ll still have access to the studio.
