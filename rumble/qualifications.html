Franchise Qualifications

For someone buying the existing Rumble Alpharetta studio, these are the qualifications and conditions stated in Rumble’s 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document and Franchise Agreement.

  1. 1

    Rumble must approve the buyer in writing.

    The franchise, studio assets, lease, ownership, or control cannot be transferred without Rumble’s prior written consent.

  2. 2

    The buyer must pass satisfactory background and credit checks.

    Rumble may review bankruptcy history, criminal history, litigation history, OFAC screening, and politically exposed-person screening.

  3. 3

    The buyer must have a good credit rating.

    The agreement requires a credit rating acceptable to Rumble.

  4. 4

    The buyer must have sufficient business experience and aptitude.

    There is no stated requirement that the buyer previously owned a gym or franchise. Rumble instead requires business experience and aptitude that meet its current standards.

  5. 5

    The buyer must have sufficient financial resources.

    The FDD requires financial resources that meet Rumble’s “then-current standards for franchisees.” The transfer section does not state a specific minimum net worth or liquid-capital requirement.

  6. 6

    The buyer must meet Rumble’s character and reputation standards.

    Rumble may consider the buyer’s character, reputation, and whether there are any conflicting business interests.

  7. 7

    Required Rumble training must be completed.

    The incoming owner or Operating Principal must complete required Owner/Operator training, and the Designated Manager must complete Studio Management Training. The 2025 FDD describes approximately 18 hours of Franchise Partner Training and 17 hours of Studio Management Training, although Rumble determines the training requirements that apply to a particular transfer.

  8. 8

    The studio must have at least one Rumble-authorized trainer.

    The incoming franchisee must have at least one Authorized Trainer before reopening or providing approved Rumble services.

  9. 9

    A $10,000 transfer fee applies.

    The 2025 FDD lists the standard franchise transfer fee as $10,000.

  10. 10

    The buyer must sign the required Rumble franchise and transfer documents.

    Rumble can require the buyer to either assume the existing franchise agreement or sign Rumble’s then-current franchise agreement for the remaining franchise term.

In simple terms

Rumble Corporate must approve the new owner. The buyer needs to pass background and credit checks, have acceptable credit and financial resources, demonstrate appropriate business experience and aptitude, complete Rumble’s required training, and sign the required franchise transfer documents. There is also a $10,000 transfer fee.

Importantly, the 2025 FDD’s transfer provisions do not publish a specific minimum net worth or liquid-capital requirement. Those financial qualifications are based on Rumble’s current franchisee approval standards.

Timing

Once there is a bona fide written offer to purchase the studio, Rumble also has contractual right-of-first-refusal provisions that may affect the timing of the transaction. The agreement provides Rumble with a period in which it may exercise those rights, so this should be confirmed with Corporate as early as possible if a transfer needs to happen quickly.
